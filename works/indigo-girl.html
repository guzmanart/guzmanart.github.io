Pastel

Indigo Girl

Medium
Pastel
Year
2017

Light on dark skin fascinates me. It reflects dozens of colors: violets, sapphires, cerulean, even vermilion. To create the undertones on Indigo Girl, I applied hard pastel according to the planes of the face, in bright blues and purples, then lighter tones in the second and third layers. The effect of inner illumination makes her appear to be her own light source.

Indigo was a slave crop of the Chesapeake Bay.
