Pastel

Hester

Medium
Pastel
Year
2018

Hester is a young woman whose hair is graying. She could be Hester Prynne from The Scarlet Letter, or a woman of the Salem witch trials. Stunned, she looks her accuser in the eye.

I blended flesh, golden and yellow ochres with unexpected English and turquoise blues, and purplish blue-greys for shading her face and collarbones.
