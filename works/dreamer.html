Dreamer (21st Century Trail of Tears)

Medium
Pastel
Year
2018

I love portraiture. I think people are the most interesting subjects to paint. DACA Dreamers reminded me of the Native Americans of the nineteenth-century Trail of Tears, so my 21st Century Trail of Tears illustrates the irony of our immigration debates.

This Dreamer is my version of a modern-day Statue of Liberty. She could be Latina, Native American or mixed, legal or undocumented. She begs the question: who is really American?
