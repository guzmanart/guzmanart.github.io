Pastel

Liberadora

Medium
Pastel
Year
2018

Shellshock. Light and shadow on her face. Liberadora is a freedom fighter in the Spanish Civil War. Aerial bombs have destroyed her town, and there is chaos all around her: a mother clinging to her bundle, a limp child, and a horse galloping through the broken bodies peek through the smoke. This is how I envision my freedom fighter of Guernica.
