Pastel

North

Medium
Pastel
Year
2017

This North Korean woman stands at the border between light and dark. Kim Jong Un's profile lurches in the shadows as light shines from her other side. Her face is mask-like, to reflect the oppressive regime, and carries the red and blue of the yin and yang on the South Korean flag.
