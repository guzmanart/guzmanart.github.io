About

Clara Guzman is a figurative artist. Her work moves between oil painting, pastel portraiture, watercolor and drawing from life, and returns again and again to the human face.

She spent a year studying painting at the School of Sacred Art in Florence, Italy, where she trained in drawing and oil painting in the classical tradition.

Before Florence, her pastel portraits explored identity, history and light on skin, from Indigo Girl to Dreamer. She studied at Harvard College.
